
## Objective

Ensure that each character is visually distinguishable in the VR environment and can be quickly identified by the player during gameplay.

## 1. Silhouette Clarity

### Observations

- Characters have clearly defined shapes that stand out against the environment  
- Each unit type appears visually distinct when viewed from a distance  
- No overlapping silhouettes that cause confusion between units  

### VR Validation

- In VR perspective, characters remain identifiable even during movement  
- Silhouettes are readable without requiring close inspection  

### Result

 Clear silhouette maintained across all visible characters  
 No visual ambiguity observed  


## 2. Scale & Proportion 

### Observations

- Characters are consistently scaled relative to the battlefield  
- Differences in size between units are noticeable  
- Proportions (height, width, body structure) vary between characters  

### VR Validation

- Scale feels natural in VR space (no oversized or undersized units)  
- Characters are easy to distinguish based on physical presence  

### Result

 Distinct scale and proportions confirmed  
 Proper spatial consistency maintained

## 3. Visual Distinction Between Characters

### Observations

- Characters differ in:
  - Model structure  
  - Movement style  
  - Combat posture

### VR Validation

- Player can visually separate units even in multi-character combat  
- No confusion between allied/enemy units based on appearance  

### Result

 Strong visual differentiation across characters  
 No duplication or ambiguity observed

 Recognition requirement successfully met
