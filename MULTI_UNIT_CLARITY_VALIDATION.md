## 1. Objective

Validate combat readability when 10–20 units are active simultaneously.

Ensure:
- No visual clutter
- Player can track units clearly
- No overlapping confusion


## 2. Test Setup

- 10–20 units spawned simultaneously
- Mixed Karma types (Aggressive, Disciplined, Risk-based)
- Full combat engagement scenarios
- Standard battlefield conditions


## 3. Validation Results

### Visual Clutter

- Multiple units on screen remain visually clear
- VFX is controlled and does not overwhelm the scene

Result:
-  No visual clutter observed
-  Effects remain readable and minimal



### Unit Tracking

- Player can easily follow:
  - Individual units
  - Movement directions
  - Combat interactions

Result:
-  Units are consistently trackable
-  No loss of visual focus during combat


### Overlapping & Confusion

- Units maintain readable spacing during combat
- No identity confusion even in close encounters

Result:
-  No overlapping confusion
-  Clear unit distinction maintained
